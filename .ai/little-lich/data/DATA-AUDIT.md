# Data audit and first refinement pass

Source files (your old game data, kept unchanged in `data/source/`): `units.json` (18 units), `research.json` (4 tomes, 78 nodes), `build.json` (8 buildings).

Refined files (first pass, in `data/refined/`): `units.json`, `research/necromancy.json`, `research/infusion.json`, `research/cryptmagic.json`, `research/spidersorcery.json`, `buildings.json`, `resources.json`. They follow the conventions in `phases/phase-10-unit-json.md` and `phase-11-item-json.md` (stable snake_case ids, `requires` lists, costs keyed by resource id). They are generated by a script from the source files, so nothing was lost; every refined entry carries a `review` flag where a human decision is still needed.

## What checked out
- All three files are valid JSON.
- No broken references: every research `Requirements` and `Next` name exists, every unit `UnlockResearch` points to a real research node.
- No duplicate research names and no cycles in the requirement graph. Each tome has exactly one root (Summoning, Basic Infusion, Necro Management, Web Slinger).
- All 8 buildings use the same four level tiers (1-5, 6-10, 11-15, 16-20), so one cost formula covers them.

## Fixes already applied in the refined files
- Typos: `Necormancer` to `Necromancer`, `Expanded Minons` to `Expanded Minions`, `Elemental Professional` to `Elemental Proficiency` (check that this is the intended word).
- `Skeleton Archer` had the Skeleton Warrior description ("A boney warrior..."). Changed to "A boney archer...". Check `Skeleton Mage` too (it says mage, so it is fine).
- Names became stable ids (`skeleton_archer`, `dark_recruitment`). Names stay for display only.
- Costs use resource ids (`mana`, `souls`, `bones`...). Art paths are kept under `art` so the Godot project can still load them.
- Building `SummoningStation` became `summoning_circle` and `MagicStorage` became `storage`, matching the design doc. The old UI called it "Magical Storage".
- `Next` in research was dropped. It is redundant with `requires` and 19 of 44 entries disagree with it (see below).

## Problems found (need a decision)

### 1. Research layout is not finished
- 41 nodes have `Position [0,0]` (all of Crypt Magic and Spider Sorcery, plus 14 Necromancy and 9 Infusion nodes). 39 of them are not tree roots and are flagged `needs_layout`. They need real positions for the tree view.
- Several nodes share one position: Necromancy has four nodes at (2,3) and two at (0,2) and (1,5); Infusion has five at (2,1). They would overlap in the tree.
- Positions are a small grid of 3 to 4 columns. The new tree (Path of Exile 2 style, large nodes, pan and zoom) needs a bigger layout. Proposal: keep your grid as hints and let a layout script (or you) place nodes; flagged as `needs_layout`.

### 2. `Next` disagrees with `Requirements` (19 entries)
Examples: `Soul Well` and `Undead Market` both say Next is `Skeleton Archer`, which is copy-paste. `Necrotic Harvest` says Next is `Soul Harvesting`, which would make a loop if it were a requirement. Refined files use `requires` only.

### 3. Costs that go down the tree (14 nodes cost less than something they require)
Examples: `Dark Empowerment` 2,500 after `Expanded Minions` 3,750; `Summoning Power` 250 after `Soul Well` 1,250; `Cheap Bones` and `Experienced Bones` 250 after `Expanded Army` 12,500. Many of these are the repeatable "+x% per level" nodes that have a placeholder 250. Decide: are those level nodes meant to be cheap, or is 250 a placeholder? Also 250 is the cost on 22 of the 23 repeatable nodes (Dark Expansion 1 is 300), so it is almost certainly a placeholder.

### 4. Effects are text only
64 of 78 nodes describe their effect only in the description ("+10 max necromancers. Necromancers cost 25% less to summon."). Only the 14 `unlock_unit` effects could be derived automatically from the units file. The rest need structured effects (`stat_mod`, `unlock_building`, `add_summon_slot`, `add_research_bench`...) before the game can apply them. This is real work and is the biggest item in research. Flagged `effects_not_structured`.

### 5. Repeatable nodes
23 nodes have `MaxLevel` (1 to 999). The cost per level is not defined (one `UnlockCost`). Decide whether cost grows per level (and by what formula), and what `TimeMultiplier` (only `Mage Hand 1`) means.

### 6. Unit model differs from the design doc
- The design says units are built from a base plus a weapon, armour and items in the Unit Builder, with 8 formation slots and 3 actions per section. Your data has fixed unit types: squads of a given `UnitSize`, a barracks cap (`Max`), one `Large` unit (Skeleton Golem takes 2 slots), and gear (`Sword`, `Bow`, `Staff`) as a **summon cost** on the Skeleton Warrior, Archer and Mage.
- There is no armour, no per-unit action list or cooldowns, and no loadouts.
- Decide: keep these fixed unit types for v0.1 (simpler, matches the data), and treat the weapon/armour/loadout Unit Builder as a later feature, or change the data to fit the design. Phases 9 and 10 depend on this answer.

### 7. Numbers are on the old game's scale
- Necromancer `Max` is 5 and research adds +5, +10, +20, +50 and "doubles your max necromancers". The design assumed up to 100 billion. Decide the real scale for v0.1. Everything (storage caps, costs, UI abbreviations, big-number type in phase 1) depends on it.
- Stat scales: HP 25 to 900, ATK 3 to 50, `SPD` 0.5 to 5. It is not stated whether `SPD` is seconds between attacks or an attack rate. The design uses cooldowns in seconds, so this must be confirmed.
- `Duration` on statuses and passives (Stun 1, Poison 5, Aggro 15) has no unit in the data. Seconds or ticks?

### 8. Insight versus Focus
The research data uses both: `Increased Focus` "+4 Focus", `Spider Eyes` "+3 Focus", `Spell Slinger` "+10 Insight". Same stat, two names. Pick one (the UI mockups use Insight).

### 9. Building costs
- Costs use the formula `base x multiplier^(level - tier start)` rounded to 2 significant figures. The refined `buildings.json` includes a generated table for levels 1 to 20 so you can check it.
- One real inconsistency: **Magic Storage** costs drop between tiers. Fire and Earth are 1,600 at level 5 and 400 at level 6. The first tier's multiplier of 2 on a base of 100 versus the second tier base of 400 looks wrong.
- Mana Well levels 1 to 5 jump about 2.3x per level, then 1.5x, 1.6x, 1.4x. The curve is plausible but the first tier is steep relative to the others. Check this feels right in play.
- Building level cap is 20 here. The research `Upgraded Infusions` adds "+10 max upgrade level for nodes and buildings", so caps are meant to rise. The data has no cap field.
- The `build` cost is a one-off cost to unlock the building. The new UI shows only upgrades. Confirm that buildings are built (once) and then upgraded.
- `Mana Well` upgrades cost Water and Earth, which come from the Infuser. Intended dependency: Mana Well upgrades need elements, so the Infuser must be built first. This matters for the order in which things unlock.

### 10. Naming and structure
- Resources: `Coin`, `Mana`, `Souls`, `Bones`, `Flesh`, `Fire`, `Water`, `Earth`, `Air` are resources. `Sword`, `Bow`, `Staff` are items used in costs. Phase 11 should say whether gear items sit in Storage with resources or in inventory. The refined `resources.json` marks them `gear_item`.
- The research tomes are `Necromancy`, `Infusion`, `CryptMagic` and `SpiderSorcery`. The design doc named different tomes (Necromancy 1, Holy Magic, Beast Magic...). The doc needs updating, and `phase-07` says "Necromancy 1 only", while your data has four tomes.
- `ResearchBench` is listed with the buildings but is not a Workshop building. Treat it as the bench system (phase 7).
- Unit `Tags` appear on 10 of 18 units (Wisp, Skeleton). The other 8 have none. Probably only needed where research or spells target a family ("all skeleton units").

## Counts, for reference
- Units: 1 resource unit, 16 squads, 1 large. 9 melee, 4 ranged, 2 AOE, 2 healer.
- Research nodes: Necromancy 33, Infusion 27, Crypt Magic 9, Spider Sorcery 9.
- Resource ids used: mana, souls, bones, flesh, coin, fire, water, earth, air, sword, bow, staff.

## Suggested order for refining
1. Decide the number scale (item 7) and the unit model (item 6). They drive everything else.
2. Fix the placeholders: the 250 costs and the building cost anomaly (items 3, 5, 9).
3. Structure the effects (item 4), starting with Necromancy since v0.1 only needs that tome.
4. Lay out the research trees (item 1).
5. Align the design doc and phases to the data (tomes, unit model, building names).
